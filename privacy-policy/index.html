Privacy Policy

This policy explains how OmniTools handles tool inputs, analytics, advertising technologies, browser storage and contact information.

Effective Date: October 6, 2026 | Last Updated: October 6, 2026

At OmniTools (accessible from https://www.omnitechtools.com/), safeguarding your privacy and data autonomy is our foundational commitment. This document articulates what technical data is processed, how advertising cookies operate, and how you can manage your privacy rights under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy frameworks.

1. Zero-Server Data Processing (Tool Inputs)

Unlike conventional utility websites, OmniTools utilizes client-side architecture. When you upload or paste data into our tools—including images, PDF documents, JSON payloads, passwords, SQL queries, or JWT authentication tokens—all computation occurs exclusively in your device's browser memory (V8/JavaScriptCore). Your input data is never transmitted across the internet to our servers, never stored in remote databases, and never inspected by human personnel. When you close the browser tab, all temporary calculation buffers are instantly cleared from RAM.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

To keep our tools completely free and accessible without subscription paywalls, OmniTools displays programmatic advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense.

  • Use of Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies and web beacons to serve advertisements on OmniTools.
  • DoubleClick / DART Cookie: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads to our users based on their prior visits to OmniTools and/or other websites across the Internet.
  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry-Wide Opt-Out: You may also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out page or the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) portal at www.aboutads.info/choices/ (or for European users, www.youronlinechoices.eu/).

3. Web Analytics & Aggregated Telemetry

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID: G-T0HG80BJQ2) to understand aggregate traffic patterns, such as which tools are most utilized, average session durations, and general device/browser screen resolutions. Google Analytics collects anonymized IP addresses, approximate geographic location, referring URLs, and browser user-agent headers. This telemetry is strictly used to diagnose site reliability, enhance Core Web Vitals, and guide tool development.

4. Local Browser Storage (Preferences)

OmniTools may use your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save non-identifiable user preferences, such as light/dark interface mode, high-contrast dyslexic font settings, and recent unit conversion configurations. These tokens remain strictly on your physical device and are never synchronized with external servers.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights (European Economic Area)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), visitors located in the EEA and UK possess comprehensive data rights, including the right to access, rectify, restrict, or erase any personal information we may hold (such as direct correspondence via email). Because our tools do not collect personal identities or store tool inputs, no personal tool data is held to be erased. For any inquiries regarding analytics or email support records, contact us at privacy@omnitechtools.com.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to request disclosure of categories of personal information collected, request deletion of personal information, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. OmniTools does not sell personal information of any consumer to third parties.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

OmniTools does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that OmniTools inadvertently possesses personal information of a child under 13, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records from our systems.

8. Policy Updates & Contact Details

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect evolving regulatory frameworks or technological features. Changes become effective immediately upon publication on this page. For questions or privacy compliance requests, email our data governance officer at privacy@omnitechtools.com.
