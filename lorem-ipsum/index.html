Free Lorem Ipsum Generator - The Designer Guide to Placeholder Text

1. What is Lorem Ipsum and Where Did It Come From?

Lorem ipsum is scrambled Latin used as placeholder text in design and publishing. Despite sounding like classical literature, the standard passage is actually a jumbled fragment from de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum, a philosophical work written by Cicero in 45 BC. A typesetter in the 1500s scrambled the words to create specimen text, and the tradition has survived five centuries into the digital age.

The genius of lorem ipsum is that it mimics the natural rhythm of English-language text, with realistic word lengths and letter distribution, without being readable. When a client looks at a layout filled with real copy, they read the copy. When they look at lorem ipsum, they see the design. That single shift in attention is why every design tool, from Figma to InDesign, ships with a placeholder generator built in.

3. When to Use Placeholder Text (and When Not To)

Placeholder text is perfect during wireframing, visual design and early prototyping. It lets you stress-test a layout against realistic content volume: does the card break with a long headline, does the sidebar wrap gracefully, does the footer cope with three columns of links? Answering these questions with dummy text is faster and cheaper than waiting for final copy.

The danger zone is presenting designs to clients too late in the process with lorem ipsum still in place, or worse, shipping it to production. Real content always behaves differently from placeholder text: real headlines are longer, real paragraphs vary wildly, and real calls to action need actual words. Treat lorem ipsum as scaffolding, not structure, and always do a final pass with genuine copy before launch.

4. Placeholder Text Best Practices for Professionals

Match the volume of placeholder text to the volume you expect in reality. If the final homepage hero will carry a twelve-word headline, do not design around four words of lorem ipsum. Generate enough paragraphs to simulate the longest realistic case, then the shortest, and check the layout survives both.

Vary your placeholder content deliberately. Use short punchy paragraphs for marketing sections and dense blocks for article layouts. Test edge cases: a one-word button label, a five-line navigation item, an empty state with no content at all. Experienced designers know that layouts rarely fail with average content; they fail at the extremes, and placeholder text is the cheapest way to find those failures early.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is lorem ipsum real Latin?

The words are genuine Latin drawn from a work by Cicero, but the standard passage has been deliberately scrambled and altered over centuries, so it does not translate into a coherent sentence. That is precisely the point: it looks like language without distracting anyone with meaning.

Q: How much placeholder text should I generate for a mockup?

Generate slightly more than you expect the real content to occupy. If the final page will have three paragraphs of body copy, test with five. Layouts that survive a content overflow gracefully will handle anything the client eventually writes, while layouts designed to the minimum break the moment real copy arrives.

Q: Can I use this generator for commercial client work?

Absolutely. The generated text is the centuries-old public-domain lorem ipsum passage, free for any use including commercial projects, client presentations and shipped templates. There are no licences, attributions or restrictions.

Q: Why not just type random words or paste real articles?

Random words have unnatural letter patterns that make text blocks look wrong, distorting your typographic judgement. Real articles pull attention to their content, which defeats the purpose of a design review. Lorem ipsum hits the sweet spot: visually realistic, intellectually invisible.

Q: Does the generator work without an internet connection?

Yes. The text is assembled locally in your browser with JavaScript. Once the page has loaded you can go offline entirely and keep generating paragraphs, sentences and words without interruption.
